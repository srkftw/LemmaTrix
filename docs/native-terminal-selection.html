Bounded native terminal selection

Campaign010 extends the existing circuit owner with eight approved public peer slots and authenticated terminal retirement. Relay selection still uses the first three approved, distinct relay identities and families. Slots 3–7 can hold separately reviewed mailbox or Feed terminal certificates. The selected provider certificate remains pinned; choosing another terminal does not change that provider's independently approved identity.

Enrollment operations 22, 23 and 24 accept peer slots 0–7. Operation 26 exports an explicitly selected public peer certificate, with slot 8 selecting the local certificate. Legacy operation 25 retains its original slots 0–3 and local sentinel 4. These operations never export private keys. The normal commands use the extended operation:

./lt onion --socket PERSONAL.sock stage mailbox.cert --peer 3
./lt onion --socket PERSONAL.sock approve --peer 3 --fingerprint EXACT_SHA256
./lt onion --socket PERSONAL.sock export --peer 3 --output mailbox.cert

The terminal scheduler keeps four caller-owned client profiles and serializes their selected streams over one approved three-hop route. The terminal owner finishes or explicitly cancels an in-flight request before asking the circuit owner to end the selected stream. An authenticated RELAY_END carrying reason 6 requests retirement. The final relay closes only that adjacent terminal channel, replies with authenticated RELAY_END reason 6 and retires only the matching stream. The client waits for that acknowledgement before establishing a fresh stream and fresh inner Noise session for the next profile.

Responses already in flight can precede the acknowledgement. CONNECTED and DATA for the exact ending stream are authenticated, charged and drained without being exposed to another profile. A changed stream identifier fails closed. Per-stream credit is erased only after the ordered END exchange. Circuit windows, rolling digests, cipher counters, hop keys and signed lifetime byte/work allowances continue unchanged; terminal reselection cannot renew exhausted route authority.

If the final relay has no free adjacent channel, or its selected terminal connection is refused, it returns authenticated RELAY_END reason 4 for that pending BEGIN. The client reports busy/unavailable while preserving the route. A failed first-hop connection returns unavailable immediately after the adapter reports its actual failed CONNECT or LISTEN outcome; it does not wait for the full handshake deadline. No path substitutes direct transport for a routed request.

NodeStore schema 2 and 3 remain unchanged. Explicit recovery restores only the approved local static credentials, certificate and supported opaque identity seal. Peer certificates are deliberately re-enrolled through normal native review after restart. Live peer grants, stream identifiers, circuit keys and process handles are never recovered from those records.
